Questions and answers

Try Fuel & Frame now: join the public beta on TestFlight.
Is Fuel & Frame on the App Store?

Not yet — it's in public beta. Join the beta on TestFlight to try it now; the App Store release is coming soon.

How do I join the beta?

Open the TestFlight link on your iPhone, install TestFlight if you don't have it, then tap Accept and Install. You can send feedback from inside TestFlight or the app.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 27 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 27 for the watch app. It also runs on iPad, where wide screens get two columns.

Do I need a coach?

No. App Access is the full app on your own, with targets calculated for you. You can add a coach later — see plans.

How much does it cost?

App Access is $14.99 a month; coached plans start at $24.99. See plans for everything each one includes.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Weight, body composition, sleep, steps, workouts and heart rate come in from Apple Health, and what you log can be saved back to it. You choose what to share.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything you log is saved on your iPhone and sent as soon as you're back online.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data. Glucose readings never leave your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fuel & Frame. Your history stays in your account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. If you can't sign in, email privacy@basaltlabsapps.com.
