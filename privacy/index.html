Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Fuel & Frame ("the App") is operated by BASALT LABS LLC ("we", "us"). It helps you track nutrition, training, fasting and progress — on your own, or with a coach if you choose a coached plan — and lets you connect with friends. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

In plain terms: your data is used only to run the App for you. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share Apple Health data with anyone you haven't chosen. The App contains no advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, a unique @username, and phone number (optional). If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email Apple shares with us.
  • Profile and intake: sex, date of birth, height, goal, activity level and food preferences, used to work out your targets.
  • Body measurements: weight, body fat percentage, lean mass and tape measurements, entered by you or synced from Apple Health.
  • Nutrition: food, recipes, water and drinks you log, including caffeine and alcohol, with quantities and nutrients.
  • Fitness: workouts, sets, reps, weights and session notes you log, plus workouts, heart rate, steps, distance and activity from Apple Health or Apple Watch.
  • Fasting, sleep and check-ins: fasting sessions, sleep, weekly check-ins and how you're feeling.
  • Goals and experiments: the two-week experiments you run, your settings and preferences (such as units, theme and theme schedule), and, if you add it, "Your why": a few words, a photo or a voice note about why you started, which only you can see.
  • Coach moments: on coached plans, short voice messages your coach records for you, played when you reach a milestone.
  • Feedback: anything you send us through Send Feedback in the App.
  • Photos and voice notes: a profile photo, any workout photos you choose to share, and your "Your why" photo or voice note. Location and camera details are removed on your device before a photo is uploaded.
  • Messages: on plans that include a coach, the messages and attachments you exchange with your coach, and any requests you send them (for example, to move a workout to another day).
  • Daily goals and streaks: which of your daily goals (Fuel, Train and Hydrate) you hit, and which days were "on track", so your streaks are the same on every device.
  • Friends: your friend connections, requests, the workout moments and streak milestones you share, reactions, and any reports you make.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send the notifications you've turned on.
  • Subscription: which plan you're on and whether it's active. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store your card details.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Glucose: if you use a glucose monitor that writes to Apple Health, the App reads your glucose readings to show how you respond to meals. These readings and the results are worked out and kept on your iPhone; they are not sent to our servers or to your coach.
  • Readiness: resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep from Apple Health are used on your iPhone to suggest how hard to train.
  • Approximate location: if you turn on a theme schedule that follows sunrise and sunset, the App asks for your approximate location (about 10 km) to work out those times. It stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us.
  • "Type what you ate": the text you type is read on your iPhone to find matching foods.

How we use it

To run the App for you: calculating your targets, showing your progress and history, sending the reminders you've chosen, delivering your plan if you have a coach, and powering the friends features you opt into. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles from it.

Who can see it

  • You can see everything you've logged.
  • Your coach, only if you're on a coached plan (Nutrition, Nutrition + Training or Full Coaching), can see your logs, measurements and check-ins in order to coach you. On App Access, no coach sees your data.
  • Friends see only what you choose to share, and only after you've both accepted the friendship. By default they see your @username, profile photo and the workouts you share, and, if you share streaks, your streak counts, awards and the days you've both kept going together. Your first name is hidden unless you turn it on. Friends never see your weight, body measurements or food. You can change these choices in Sharing settings, and remove or block a friend at any time.
  • People who aren't your friends see only your @username (for example in search or a friend request).
  • Coaches never see your friends activity or shared moments.

These rules are enforced in our database (row-level security), not just in the App's screens.

Apple Wallet invite card

If you add your invite card to Apple Wallet, the card holds your @username, your first name if you've chosen to show it, your photo if you've chosen to include it, and a QR code friends can scan to add you. The card is created when you ask for it and stays on your device; anyone you show it to can see what's on it.

Third parties

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and server functions. It processes data only on our behalf.
  • Apple processes subscription payments, Sign in with Apple, push notifications and Apple Wallet passes. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those services.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts are public food databases. Only your search terms or the barcode you scan are sent — never personal or health data.
  • Exercise videos: to show how-to videos, our server asks a licensed exercise video library for videos by exercise name only. No personal or health data is sent.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email, so messages you send us are stored there.

Apple Health data specifically

If you grant permission, the App reads weight, height, body composition, waist, sleep, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, distance, activity, glucose, water and nutrition, and writes back weigh-ins, waist, water and the food and workouts you log in the App. You choose each type in the Health permission screen and can change it at any time in the Health app. In line with Apple's requirements:

  • We do not use Health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not sell Health data to data brokers or anyone else.
  • We do not share Health data with third parties, except with your coach when you're on a coached plan. Glucose readings are never shared, not even with your coach.
  • Health data is never shared with friends.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the App (Profile → Delete Account). This removes your profile, logs, photos, voice notes, friend connections and shared moments, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Records Apple keeps about your purchases are managed by Apple. You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact below), for example if you can no longer sign in.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to ask for a copy or deletion of your data, email privacy@basaltlabsapps.com. For help with the App, email support@basaltlabsapps.com or go to Profile → Send Feedback in the App.
