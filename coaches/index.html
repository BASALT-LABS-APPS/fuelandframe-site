For coaches

Coach your clients where they already log.

Your clients track food, training and progress in Fuel & Frame. You see it all, build their plans and keep in touch — in the same app.

Coach with Fuel & Frame

Your clients at a glance

See who needs you today — missed check-ins, quiet weeks and milestones — and approve new clients when they sign up.

Programs and workouts

Build programs with supersets, circuits and timed work, assign them in a tap and see every session your clients log.

Meal plans and recipes

Write meal plans and recipes your clients can log in one tap, and set their targets or let the app calculate them.

Check-ins and feedback

Review weekly check-ins, comment on logs and adjust targets and programs as your clients progress.

Messages

Message clients directly, with quick replies for the things you say every week.

Milestone notes

Record a short voice note that plays when a client reaches a milestone you set.
