Fuel & Frame Support

Email support@basaltlabsapps.com and we'll reply within two business days. In the app you can also go to Profile → Send Feedback.

Account

I can't sign in.

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or Sign in with Apple if you signed up with it. Still stuck? Email us from the address on your account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile, logs, photos and friend connections. If you can't sign in, email privacy@basaltlabsapps.com and we'll delete it for you.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fuel & Frame. In the app, Profile → Plan shows your current plan and lets you upgrade.

I paid but the app doesn't show my plan.

Open the plans screen and tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't show, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

My workouts, weight or steps aren't showing.

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Fuel & Frame and turn on the data you want to share, then open Fuel & Frame again.

Glucose insights don't appear.

They need a glucose monitor that writes to Apple Health, and permission for Blood Glucose in the Health app. Readings stay on your iPhone.

Everyday use

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything you log is saved on your iPhone and sent when you're back online.

Can I change the colours?

Yes, in Profile → Appearance, including a schedule that changes the theme by day of the week or at sunset.

Open the TestFlight link on your iPhone and tap Accept and Install.
